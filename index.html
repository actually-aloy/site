What does trans mean?

Being transgender is about gender identity — your own internal sense of being a man, a woman, both, neither, or something else. A trans person's gender identity doesn't match the sex they were assigned at birth, so they may transition (socially, medically, or both) to live as their actual gender.

Being gay is about sexual orientation — who you're romantically or sexually attracted to. It has nothing to do with gender identity.

These are two separate parts of a person: identity (who you are) vs. orientation (who you're attracted to). A trans person can be straight, gay, bisexual, or any other orientation, just like anyone else. Being trans doesn't say anything about who someone is attracted to, and being gay doesn't say anything about someone's gender identity.
